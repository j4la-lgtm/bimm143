on top of Molatar, filter it for `everything' and look at the HOH
sequence. The residue number this water molecule has is 301 (HOH 301).

Q14. What do you note about this plot? Are the black and colored lines
similar or different? Where do you think they differ most and why?

and the smaller peaks. However, the colored lines do show much more
higher peaks. I think they differ the most at these peaks, probably to
show fluctuations as the residue numbers get higher. The lines and peaks
also help to observe the differences and similarities between protein
structures.
